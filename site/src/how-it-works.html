How it works

Your users tap the Feedback button in the corner of your app. It opens three options: report a bug, share an idea or write a review. They pick one, add a few words, and it is on your feedback board.

OptionWhat your users doWho sees it
!Report a bug Tell you something does not work. They can add a screenshot, which goes on the board once they tick that everyone may see it, and your app's version comes along if you pass it in. Everyone, screenshot too Version: only you
+Share an idea Suggest something your app is missing. Similar ideas show up while they type, so they can vote for one that is already there instead of posting it twice. Everyone
★Write a review Give one to five stars and a few words. The average rating sits at the top of the board. One review per wallet. Everyone
✓Your answer Planned, Done or Not doing, with a line of your own under the post. It is on the board, so nobody needs an inbox. Done Not doing

Three ways to show it

Pick one with mode. On a phone the board covers the screen; on a wider screen it opens as a panel in the corner.

A button in the corner

mode="floating". A small round button that opens on the tiles. The "paste one line and done" route.

Part of your page

mode="inline". The board fills the element you put it in, for a feedback screen of your own.

From your own menu

mode="overlay", or open() from a menu item you already have. The board, straight away.
