Why PleaseNoBugs

Your users talk. You answer. Everyone sees it.

A free, open-source feedback widget for apps built on Nimiq. Bugs, ideas and reviews, right inside your app.

Feedback without the black hole

Your users know what's broken and what's missing. They rarely tell you, and when they do, it vanishes in a chat or an inbox.

With the widget they say it where they already are: in your app, signing with the wallet they already have. Others vote. You answer underneath, in public.

Why Nimiq developers use it

  • Your data, your server. Feedback lives on a server you run. Posts, wallets and names never leave it. Only a daily count of bugs and ideas does, and you can switch that off.
  • No sign-up. Users sign with their Nimiq wallet. No email, no password.
  • Votes show what matters. The most wanted ideas and the most annoying bugs rise to the top. Duplicates get caught while typing.
  • You own the board. Your wallet address is the owner. Your answer, the status and the fix version sit under the post.

Free and MIT licensed, in Nimiq's look, light or dark. Try it in Make it yours →

The goal

Make feedback effortless for users and actionable for developers.

Add it in 3 steps

A server, a script and one tag. Paste it in, and your app has a feedback board.

  1. Start your server

    Your board lives on a server of your own. On Railway that is one button and three fields; Node and Docker work too. It prints your project id and address.

    Set up a server

  2. Paste the script and the tag

    <script src="https://unpkg.com/pleasenobugs@%VERSION%"></script>
<please-no-bugs
  project="YOUR_PROJECT_ID"
  server="https://YOUR_SERVER"
  mode="floating"
></please-no-bugs>

    With a bundler: npm install pleasenobugs, then import 'pleasenobugs' once.

  3. Make it yours

    A button in the corner (floating), on a page of your own (inline), or over your page (overlay). Pick that, and light or dark, in Make it yours. It writes the code for you, or a prompt for your AI agent.
